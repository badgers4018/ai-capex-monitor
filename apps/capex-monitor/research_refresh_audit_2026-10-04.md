## Data-quality comparison

The scheduled batch is complete but differs materially from this dated, source-linked pass. Examples:

- It treats earlier quarterly results or estimates as fresh Oct. 4 news in several records.
- It gives generic attributions such as “Industry Infrastructure Report” and “Internal Memo & Financial Guidance Update,” rather than durable source URLs.
- The audited findings distinguish disclosed facts, forward-looking company statements, proposals pending approval, and neutral no-development outcomes.

## Controls applied

- **Time:** Source publication dates were constrained to Sep. 27–Oct. 4, 2026; Meta is intentionally neutral because the issuer archive had no qualifying release in the window.
- **Basis:** “Signal” refers only to the AI infrastructure capex thesis, not a security rating. Forward-looking revenue/capacity claims are labeled as such.
- **Sources & confidence:** Primary company, investor-relations, municipal, and Google Cloud materials were used wherever available; Reuters was used for the Broadcom/Anthropic financing because it reports from an IPO prospectus.
- **Replacement safety:** The audit has not altered the existing scheduled data. A future correction should **replace** the existing ten rows rather than append a third batch.
