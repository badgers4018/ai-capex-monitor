## User-approved brand decisions

1. **Brand hierarchy:** Capital Misallocation is primary. AI Capex Monitor is its product descriptor.
2. **Default lockup:** `Capital Misallocation - AI Capex Monitor` (hyphen to be typographically refined in UI as an en dash where appropriate).
3. **Language:** Thesis-first tone may be borrowed, but exact long-short titles “The Multiplicative Indictment” and “The Causal Barbell” may not be reused.
4. **Selected hero narrative:** “A trillion-dollar buildout is now searching for its economic clearing price.”
5. **Art direction:** Full **adaptive dual theme**, not a mere color inversion.
   - Editorial Paper is default for new visitors.
   - Dark Research is a complete intentional counterpart.
   - Persist visitor preference after selection.
6. **Cyan:** semantic-only signal for live state, evidence, or infrastructure status—never a core decorative brand color.
7. **Texture:** clean paper-like editorial surfaces; clean dark ink surfaces. A very subtle grid may appear only in dense data workspaces.
8. **Typography:** Newsreader + IBM Plex Sans + IBM Plex Mono (tables, tickers, dense data).
9. **Voice:** contrarian, sharp, thesis-forward, while claims remain evidence-linked.
10. **Vocabulary:** research language (thesis, evidence, readout, appendix, assumptions) on editorial surfaces; conventional labels on high-frequency utility controls.
11. **Motion:** user-selectable **Quiet / Standard / Dynamic**, defaulting to adaptive editorial behavior; full reduced-motion support.
12. **Scope:** all visitor-facing surfaces: landing, navigation, dashboard, cards, charts/tables, scenario/portfolio tools, TradingView, AI-search lock/upsell, email, favicon, browser/page metadata, and social preview treatment. Exclude visual styling of downloaded PDF/CSV exports for this pass.
13. **Rollout:** create the brand book first; then redesign a flagship surface (landing + dashboard header) for user review; only then extend to full product.
14. **Reference hierarchy:** the three Capital Misallocation properties are source-of-truth. Avoid glossy fintech, generic SaaS, neon crypto, and Bloomberg-lookalike design.
15. **Ecosystem navigation:** compact Research menu and footer block link the wider Capital Misallocation research network.
