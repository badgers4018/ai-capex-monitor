## Data protocol

1. **Adjusted closes:** Yahoo Finance daily adjusted closes, collected through the Manus Data API, are used to calculate Pearson correlations from aligned daily returns over the six-month / 180-calendar-day window.
2. **Market capitalization:** Yahoo Finance trailing market-cap observations determine ranks within this selection.
3. **Scenario work:** Council classifications are qualitative *capex-thesis* views—not price targets—using the three existing cases: bull, base, and bear.
4. **Scope:** All securities are public and U.S.-listed or U.S.-traded ADRs. No personal holdings are evaluated.

*This research universe is for analysis only, not personalized financial advice.*
