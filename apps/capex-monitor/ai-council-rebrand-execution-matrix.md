# AI Council — Capital Misallocation Rebrand Execution Matrix

**Purpose:** Convert the approved five-role brand-council synthesis and product brand book into a fast, complete implementation pass for **Capital Misallocation — AI Capex Monitor**.

**Governing decisions:**

- Capital Misallocation is the parent brand; **AI Capex Monitor** is the product descriptor.
- Editorial Paper is the persisted first-visit default; Dark Research is a deliberate alternate environment.
- The approved lead proposition is: **“A trillion-dollar buildout is now searching for its economic clearing price.”**
- Cyan may only signal live, verified, or infrastructure status. Gold is an editorial/reference signal; it is never the primary CTA.
- Newsreader, IBM Plex Sans, and IBM Plex Mono replace the prior Instrument Serif, Manrope, and JetBrains Mono system.
- The official mark is the authorized source-site PNG, used through a single replaceable asset registry.
- PDF/CSV export styling remains outside this rollout.

## Ten implementation recommendations

| # | AI Council recommendation | Execution decision |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Preserve parent-brand authority in every primary lockup. | Standardize the live lockup as **Capital Misallocation — AI Capex Monitor**. |
| 2 | Use one auditable official-mark source and tested derivatives. | Add a central brand-asset registry with ink, paper/light, favicon, and social/app derivatives. |
| 3 | Make Paper a designed default rather than a dark-dashboard inversion. | Replace global blue-black defaults with paper, ink, fine-rule, and soft-paper tokens. |
| 4 | Author Dark Research independently. | Add separately tuned dark surface, text, rule, and semantic data tokens under the existing `.dark` contract. |
| 5 | Distinguish editorial hierarchy from utility hierarchy. | Use Newsreader only for propositions/headlines; retain Plex Sans/Mono for controls, tables, and charts. |
| 6 | Give visitors control over environment and motion. | Add persistent, keyboard-operable Theme and Motion preferences with Quiet, Standard, and Dynamic modes. |
| 7 | Make network identity useful but secondary to monitoring work. | Add a compact accessible Research menu with the three approved Capital Misallocation properties. |
| 8 | Frame the landing as an argument that becomes an instrument. | Rebuild the landing around thesis → evidence → assumptions → monitor route → appendix/source access. |
| 9 | Make dashboard chrome compact and provenance-led. | Replace the oversized dashboard hero with a research command layer showing workspace, data state, methodology, and source access. |
| 10 | Preserve task completion and accessibility through the rebrand. | Retain routes/controls, add visible focus states, mobile navigation, reduced-motion overrides, and responsive validation. |

## Validation plan

1. Typecheck, unit tests, production build, and diff integrity check.
2. Capture desktop and 375px mobile landing/dashboard states in both Paper and Dark Research.
3. Verify the official mark, Research links, theme/motion persistence, focus behavior, and reduced-motion behavior.
4. Check browser console diagnostics before saving the completed project checkpoint.
